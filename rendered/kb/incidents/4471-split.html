The three problems

  • parser: day and month swap. The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so “7 April” goes out as “4 July”. The fix queued for #4471.
  • hint: the form tells people to type it wrong. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type month first, and the form reads the value day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not touch it: the hint and the form’s read have to change.
  • one-day: the date moves by a day. Not a swap. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk already listed these as oneDayMove in desk-notes; they belong to time-zone handling, not to the parser.

How this was counted

  • The cause comes from the tenant: its locale and its booking form (causeOf in #lib/kb/causes). Intake’s intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and is not used.
  • one-day comes from the desk’s own oneDayMove list; a full read of the bodies found no others.

For the desk

  • Do not close #4471 when the parser fix ships. Split it into the three tickets above.
  • The same subject lines (“wrong day on the invite”, “date is wrong”, …) appear in all three problems. Read the body before you merge.
